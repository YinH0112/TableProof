# Five-researcher trial protocol

Status: prepared; no participants recruited or results recorded.

Five consenting research users each complete six tasks: three in TableProof and three using PDF + Tabula + Excel. Randomize method order and counterbalance equivalent tables; no participant repeats a memorized answer. Tasks cover grouped headers, scientific symbols, corrections and notes/units.

Both methods must deliver correct values/spans/context, source locations and a change log. Give identical requirements, material and a short practice task. Do not help in timed tasks; record interventions and failures separately.

Independently transcribe target tables and agree on a scoring rubric first. Extractor seeds are not ground truth. Use trial-results.csv for participant code, task/method/order, duration, success, errors, missing context/sources and help requests. No names or confidential papers needed.

Compare median time on correctly completed tasks alongside completion/error rates. Continuation target: 30% lower median time without worse error rate. Report individuals and failed tasks; five people cannot establish population claims. Retain anonymous measurements and rubric version. Check voluntary repeat use a week later; Stars do not replace this evidence.
